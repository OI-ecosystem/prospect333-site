Questions

Straight answers

What kinds of goal can I ask about?

Anything where the answer is “who or what should I approach?”: funding, listings, buyers, partners, suppliers, licences, press, referrals. The method is the same whatever the subject.

How do you know a route is real?

We read the route's own page. Quotes shown in the report must appear in that page's text, and contact details must appear on it too. If a page doesn't say, the report says “unknown”.

What if nothing is found?

You'll see exactly what we looked for, where nothing usable turned up, and where to look yourself. If the report finds fewer than two routes with a working contact or apply path, we refund you in full.

Will you tell me if a deadline has passed?

Yes. We check dates on the page and flag closed rounds. If a closed round still has a live contact, we turn it into a question to ask about the next round.

Can I come back with a similar goal later?

Yes. The Update option re-checks an earlier report, concentrating on what has changed.

Do you write the messages for me?

Every route comes with a drafted first message with blanks for your details. You review it and send it yourself.

Is it guaranteed to win me a grant, listing or sale?

No. We tell you where the doors are and which look open. What happens when you knock is up to the people behind them.

What happens to my details?

We use your goal and email only to produce and send your report. We don't sell them.
