08What this tells you

Email the RIS helpdesk first to ask to be added to the council's childcare directory, because a listing helps parents find you. Next, check the Community Foundation's Making Local Life Better Fund, which accepts constituted groups with at least 3 committee members. It allows 12 weeks for assessment, so start soon. Then ask the Family Hub team and the health visiting service whether they can pass on details of local groups. Ask the council about its small grants round too, though the latest one has closed.

10Other places to look: suggestions, not checked

From general knowledge. Not read or verified by this report, and not counted as routes.

  • Ofsted (Early Years Register): Registration is usually needed for sessional care and is often a condition for being listed by the council and for funded places; check whether your set-up is exempt or needs registering. Search: "Ofsted register as early years provider sessional playgroup voluntary"
  • Early Years Alliance (formerly Pre-school Learning Alliance): National membership body for playgroups and pre-schools, offering advice, templates, sustainability guidance and signposting to funding. Search: "Early Years Alliance membership voluntary playgroup support"
  • Voluntary Action Rutland (VAR): Local support organisation for voluntary groups, which can help with funding searches, governance and promotion within Rutland. Search: "Voluntary Action Rutland funding support community groups"
  • National Lottery Community Fund (Awards for All England): Small grants open to constituted voluntary groups without needing to be a registered charity, and can cover running costs such as hall rent. Search: "National Lottery Community Fund Awards for All England apply"
