Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026. Prospect333 is run by Signal333 Ventures, Nottingham, UK. Contact: pay1709@sky.com

What we collect

  • The goal you type and any answers to the helper's questions.
  • Your email address, so we can send your report.
  • Payment details are entered on Lemon Squeezy's checkout page. We never see or store your card number; Lemon Squeezy, who sell the report to you as merchant of record, tell us that a payment was made and give us your email address and order reference.

Who processes it

  • Anthropic (AI): your goal text is sent to an Anthropic model to write search plans, judge pages and write the report. This happens through our own gateway hosted on Vercel.
  • Search and page-reading providers: search queries drawn from your goal are sent to web search services. Your email address is not sent to them.
  • Lemon Squeezy: takes payment, handles tax, receipts and refunds.
  • Upstash and Hetzner: store your order, progress and finished report, and run the research.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website and Vercel runs the goal helper. Both see ordinary web-request data such as IP address.

How we use it

To produce and deliver your report, handle payment and refunds, and answer your emails. We do not sell your data or use your goal to market to you.

Keeping it

We keep your goal, report and order details for as long as needed to deal with questions or refunds, and normally no longer than 12 months. Ask us to delete them any time by emailing the address above.

Your rights

Under UK data-protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Email us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Please don't include

Passwords, bank details, or sensitive personal information about other people in your goal.
